Privacy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

IN SHORT
  • Your entries stay on your device and in your private iCloud storage.
  • We have no access to them.
  • In leaderboards you only share your name and how many green days you have, only with the group and only with your consent.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics services.
  • You can delete all data yourself at any time.

1. Controller

The controller responsible for Sobra under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is:

Julien Adams, Switzerland. Contact by email: sobra.support@icloud.com

2. What data Sobra processes

Sobra stores only what you enter or set yourself: one of three levels per day (no alcohol, one or two drinks, three or more), your monthly limit and your reminder settings.

From these entries the app calculates statistics, streaks and medals on your device. If you join a leaderboard, your display name in that leaderboard and the number of your green days are added (see section 6). The app does not collect any other information such as your email address, location or contacts.

3. Sensitive data

Information about alcohol consumption can allow conclusions about your health and is therefore considered sensitive. That is why your entries do not leave your device and your private iCloud storage.

The only exception is leaderboards: only if you expressly agree, you share your name and the number of your green days with the group. Nobody learns which days were green, orange or red.

4. Storage and iCloud

Your data is stored locally on your device. If iCloud is enabled on the device, it is also stored in your private iCloud storage so that it remains available on your own devices.

This storage belongs to your Apple account. We can neither view nor read it. Storage in iCloud is subject to Apple’s privacy policy. Section 8 explains where the data may be held. You can turn off iCloud sync for Sobra in iOS Settings at any time.

5. What we do not do

Sobra has no user account and runs no servers of its own. The app contains no ads, no tracking or analytics services and no third-party software that transmits data. We do not share or sell any data. We do not make automated individual decisions about you.

6. Leaderboards with friends

Leaderboards are optional. They only exist if you create one yourself or join through an invitation. Before you take part, the app shows you what the group sees and asks for your consent.

For each leaderboard, the following is shared: the name of the leaderboard, your display name in it and the number of your green days in the counted period (for “No end” the last 30 days, for “Timed” since the start). Your place and the change since yesterday follow from this. Which days were green, orange or red, your limit, your medals and your year are not shared. A nickname is enough as your display name.

The leaderboard is stored using Apple iCloud sharing, in the iCloud storage of the person who leads it, and is visible to all members. We run no server for this and have no access. Taking part requires an Apple account with iCloud turned on.

Anyone who has the invitation link or the code can join the leaderboard without further confirmation, so only share them with people who should take part. To join with a code, Sobra stores only the code and the matching share link in the app’s public iCloud database, without names or numbers. The entry is deleted after 7 days.

Everything visible in a leaderboard can be seen by all members. They can pass it on, for example with a screenshot. Neither you nor we have any control over this.

If you leave a leaderboard, your name and numbers are removed from it, including on the other members’ devices the next time they sync. If a leaderboard is dissolved or the last person leaves, it is deleted for everyone.

7. Legal bases

The app processes your entries so that you can use it as you expect when downloading the app (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). They stay with you.

Sharing in leaderboards is based on your explicit consent (Art. 6(7)(a) FADP, Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time by leaving the leaderboard. This does not affect what was shared before.

We answer email enquiries based on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

8. Recipients and transfers abroad

The only recipients of your data are Apple, as the provider of iCloud and notifications, and, for leaderboards, the members of that leaderboard.

Apple may store data on servers in the USA and other countries. For users in Switzerland and the EU, Apple Distribution International Limited in Ireland is responsible. According to Apple, transfers to the USA are based on Standard Contractual Clauses. Members of a leaderboard may live in any country.

9. Notifications

If you turn on the weekly reminder, the app schedules the notification directly on your device. No data is sent to us or to third parties for this. You can turn the reminder off in the app or in iOS Settings at any time.

For leaderboards you receive notifications when someone joins, when a timed leaderboard ends, for the monthly recap, and when a leaderboard is ended or handed over to you. Apple delivers these notifications via iCloud. You can turn them off per leaderboard.

10. App Store and Apple statistics

Sobra is free. The download is handled by Apple. We do not learn who downloaded the app. Apple only provides us with aggregated statistics that do not identify individuals, such as the number of downloads per country.

If you have turned on “Share With App Developers” on your iPhone, Apple also passes on crash reports and usage statistics to us, which according to Apple are aggregated and do not identify you. You can turn this off under Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

11. Contacting us

If you write to us by email, we process your email address and the content of your message in order to answer your request. We delete the correspondence no later than 12 months after the matter is closed.

12. Retention and deletion

Your entries remain stored until you change or delete them. Data in a leaderboard remains stored until you leave it or it is dissolved. To remove all data, leave your leaderboards, delete the app and remove Sobra’s data in iOS Settings under your Apple account, iCloud, Manage Storage.

13. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase and receive your personal data, and the right to object to its processing. You can withdraw any consent you have given at any time. Because your entries are held only by you, you exercise these rights directly on your device. You can remove your information from a leaderboard at any time by leaving it. For data from an enquiry, an email to the email address above is sufficient.

You may also contact the competent supervisory authority. In Switzerland this is the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC); in the EU it is the data protection authority of your country of residence.

14. Minors

Sobra is intended for adults and is not directed at minors.

15. No medical advice

Sobra is not a medical device and does not replace advice from a doctor or therapist. If you are worried about your drinking, please contact a professional or a counselling service.

16. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the legal situation changes. The current version is available in the app and on this page.
